# Winning product criteria

- wow factor and grabbing attention — is it interesting
- does it solve a problem? | does it add value?
- product makes something better , easier or faster
- product has broad mass market appeal - it needs to have a market
- product targets a specific niche - niched down - solve one problem of specific ICP
- it should have high perceived value - value is tied to the effect of the product - the outcome the customer will get
- lightweight and easy to ship
- sell it 3x-5x the price of purchasing with a minimum of 20$-30$ per order profit , so you have money for ads
