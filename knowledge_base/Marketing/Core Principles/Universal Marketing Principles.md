## 8 desires in life:

- survival and enjoyment of life
- enjoyment of food and beverages
- freedom from pain , fear , danger
- sexual gratification - people want to look good
- confortable living conditions - it goes hand by hand with the freedom from pain , danger
- high social status - bragging right products - everyone wants to be above others
- care and protection of loved ones - kids niche ,pet niche , relationship niche - convince them your product will improve the quality of life of : their loved ones/ their pet/ their child
- social approval and belonging

## 5 levels of market awareness:

### where your customers are:

- Unaware - they don’t know anything about their problem, they don’t realize they need what you are offering (hardest to sell to , but highest volume)
- problem-aware (they know the problem , but they don’t know what to do about it) - focus on their problem, acnoledge their feelings and introduce the solution of their feelings
- solution-aware - they know they have a problem and he is looking for a solution already . He. is looking around - your ads should focus on how your solution works , and why you are better than other solutions - why you are better - competing on product
- product aware- they know your product, but they are not yet convinced to buy - show them benefits , social proof , offers , address common pbjections , answer questions they might have , reassure them it’s the best for them , why they need yours over others
- most aware- they need a final incentive to purchase (the easiest to sell to)  - discount , urgency offers, bonuses , limited time offers)

With ecomstore you need to focus on stages 2-4

When creating paid ads , think around the market awareness stage of the customer and make the ad around it.

## 5 stages of market sophistication

with ecomm most of the times it’s stages 2 to 4

- Stage 1  - no one heard of the product - you can just make a strong and bold claim and ppl will believe in it
- Stage 2 - there are products like yours - make bigger claims - include promise in time and measured
- Stage 3 - they are overfed ant they maybe even tried smtg already. You need to introduce a new unique mechanism , so they might believe , they don’t trust old solutions
- Stage 4 - build on top of that unique mechanism with bigger promises
- Stage 5 - very well known product - too much saturation . You can’t make claims. This is where lifestyle branding is the only working thing - ads that people identify. with . Create identity around the product.
