## **4. CTA:**

**A single call-to-action -** Multiple CTAs dilute focus. Top performers use binary questions or simple requests that require minimal cognitive load: 'Does this make sense?' or 'Worth a quick call?

**CTA type 1**: Use CTAs that lead to a no answer , not yes (OPTIONAL)

**CTA type 2** - straight as possible with options for next step included
”How does 3:30pm today sound? I can give you a ring at (insert number here) or send you a one-click Google Meet invite.”

**CTA TYPE 3:**  low friction easy answer questions
**“**Would you have a couple minutes to chat about this over the next few days?
**”**Would it be cool if I send over the strategy?”

## A whole different outreach strategy including LinkedIN

### **Day 1** — Email (80–120 words)

No links. No calendar. No images. Just context.

**Example:**

Subject: Quick question about {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Came across {{something specific about them}} and wanted to reach out.

We’ve been helping {{similar companies}} with {{specific outcome}}, and I had a quick thought around {{relevant angle}}.

Not sure if this is on your radar, but figured I’d send a note.

— {{your_name}}

### Day 3 — LinkedIn connection request

No note.

This is important.

They see:

“{{your_name}} wants to connect”

And often think:

“I’ve seen this name in my inbox.”

That’s the whole point.

### Day 6 — LinkedIn message

**Do not pitch again.**

**Example:**

Hey {{first_name}} — sent you an email earlier this week about {{topic}}. Thought I’d connect here as well.

That’s the entire message.

You’re just linking the dots in their head.

### Day 9 — Follow-up email (most replies happen here)

**Example**:

Subject: Re: Quick question about {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Just wanted to bump this in case it got buried.

Happy to share the idea I mentioned if it’s relevant on your end.

— {{your_name}}

## Interview method :

Instead of pitching directly, you reach out to a potential client and say, “I’d love to feature you in my upcoming blog post conversation.” It’s a value-first approach.

OR REFINE THAT - idea is just asking them for their business and experience.

I want to feature you in my blog post.

We are making a market research and we want to include you in it and on our blog post.

Your goal is not to sell something right now—it’s to start a conversation. Think of it like introducing yourself at a networking event

step 2: Based on what you learned, outline a basic proposal or plan.

- Highlight exactly how you’ll address their main pain points using AI solutions.
- Keep it simple and aligned with their priorities.

Send them the plan and ask them fro a second call where you can walk them through it if it makes sense for them.
