# Hooks

## Hooks - always tease a payoff and give it in the end

- **Visual - bright , attention grabbing**
- **Verbal- clear , concise , energetic**
- Audio maybe
- **Text - always include. Max 10 words. Use POWER words.**
- **Secondary Hook - is it possible somewhere in middle of the video**

**Question your hook should be aligned with**

- Would a total stranger stop and watch this?
- Does my hook clearly promise what the viewer will get by the end?
- Is my hook broad enough that most people would relate to it?
- Does my hook create curiosity or tension in the first second?
- Can the viewer see themselves and identify with the hook?
- Is the hook simple enough to understand in one glance or one listen?
- Is it tangible?

**Hook types:**

1.Pattern interrupt hook - gets instant attention

2. The shock value hook - start at the peak of emotion and value of the video , product  - freezes attention instantly

3. The secret reveal hook - feels like secret insider info only they get to hear

4. The retrospective list hook - 5 things i would buy in myt twenties that helped me become rich in my 30s.  - delivers value in a format people love

5. Curiosity hook - I was today years old when I found this out  - keeps them hanging on for the answer

6. This is the comparison question hook - why some people squat like this(visual) and some people like this(visual) - direct comparison  - makes them watch the video because they pick one of the compared sides
