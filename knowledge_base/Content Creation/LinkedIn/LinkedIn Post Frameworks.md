# LinkedIn Post Frameworks

The complete creative system for building LinkedIn posts: post types, outcomes, hooks, body structures, CTAs, and writing frameworks.

---

## Post Types & Weights

8 post types. Use weights as a guide for frequency — higher weight = post more often.

| Post Type | Weight | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| insightful | 25% | Reframe a belief or show a problem in a new way |
| personal | 15% | Personal story that builds trust and connection |
| lead_magnet | 15% | Build desire for a resource, drive comment/DM action |
| industry_update | 10% | Hot trend, tool, or market shift with your take |
| paradigm_shift | 10% | Challenge a widely-held belief, cause a mindset change |
| case_study | 10% | Client result with numbers — authority + social proof |
| emotional_case_study | 10% | Client story with emotional arc — relatability over metrics |
| offer_post | 5% | Direct service offer framed as a no-brainer |

---

## Outcome Strategy

Every post gets an assigned outcome that shapes the CTA and closing beat.

| Outcome | How it ends |
|---------|-------------|
| EDUCATE | Thought-provoking question or bold takeaway. No hard CTA. |
| SAVE | Reference-style content. "Save this for when you need it." |
| LEAD_MAGNET | Build desire throughout. Use CTA-A or CTA-E. |
| CONNECT | Build personal trust. "Follow for more" or "Connect with me if..." |
| KEYWORD_CTA | Drive comments. "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you..." |

Natural pairings: case_study → KEYWORD_CTA, offer_post → LEAD_MAGNET, personal → CONNECT, insightful/paradigm_shift → EDUCATE, lead_magnet → LEAD_MAGNET.

## Hook Patterns (H1–H13)

Pick the one that best fits the content. Vary — do not default to the same 1–2 patterns every time.

- **H1** · Industry doom claim: "Framer and Webflow are in massive trouble."
- **H2** · Concrete result + tool: "Claude Code booked 85 sales calls/month for a client."
- **H3** · Stat + payoff on line 2: "Startups with co-founders are 3x more likely to succeed. / I met mine at a hackathon."
- **H4** · Achievement + timeframe: "We built an AI GTM Engineer in less than 20 hours."
- **H5** · R.I.P.: "R.I.P. LinkedIn Agencies."
- **H6** · Devs have X. Now Y has Z.: "Devs have Claude Code. Now GTM teams have [Brand]."
- **H7** · Status update + backstory: "We launched 2 weeks ago. 60+ people jumped in."
- **H8** · Future prediction: "Intent signals will make or break your outbound in 2026."
- **H9** · Used-to-take-hours/now-minutes: "Building cold email campaigns used to take hours. Claude Code builds them in minutes:"
- **H10** · Identity-list contrarian: "I drink alcohol / I watch trash reality TV / ... / I take massive action." (use ≤1/month)
- **H11** · Framework declaration: "The 2026 GTM Flywheel." or "The evolution of outbound from 2010–2026."
- **H12** · Screw-it / IP giveaway: "Screw it. I built this over 2 years. Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send it all. FREE."
- **H13** · Diversification story: "We were 100% reliant on X. Y gave us a chance to diversify."

## Body Structures (S1–S10)

Commit to one structure per post.

**S1** · Setup → mechanics → tool stack → CTA
Hook fact → 1–2 lines context → "here's how" numbered/bulleted breakdown → tool list with hyphens → CTA

**S2** · Hook → personal story → bullet list of beliefs → close
Hook stat → personal moment → "Here's where we align: → → →" → punchy sign-off

**S3** · Hook → numbered building blocks → CTA
"3 things have to be true" framing → each block 2–3 lines → CTA

**S4** · Industry-frame → product reveal → integration list → CTA
Framing quote or stat → short context → arrow list → free-tier or lead-magnet CTA

**S5** · Tool-stack-by-layer:
The full stack by layer:
- data: Apollo, Clay, Prospeo
- enrich: FullEnrich, CompanyEnrich
- orchestrate: n8n, Claude Code
- action: Instantly.ai, HeyReach

**S6** · Era timeline:
2017–2019: Revenue Intelligence
↳ ZoomInfo, 6sense, Gong

2020–2022: Intent Data
↳ Bombora, UserGems, Demandbase

**S7** · Market map — categorize 50–70 tools into tiers (Favorites · Rising · Unicorns · Others). Only for big industry overview posts.

**S8** · Client case study:
"We built X for [Client] that generated [number] in [time window]."
Numbered breakdown → 3 result stats → close

**S9** · Identity-list / roast-list:
5–7 short "I do / I don't" statements flipping orthodoxy → one reframe line. (use ≤1/month)

**S10** · Problem → System → Result (primary structure for automation/ops/pipeline topics):
Business problem (1–2 lines, specific and concrete — name the exact gap or leak).
Why it happens (2–3 lines: root cause, not symptom).
The system that fixes it (3–5 lines or numbered steps).
The result (1–2 lines with a specific number).

## Insightful Post Frameworks

For insightful and paradigm_shift posts. Choose based on the topic input:
- Topic is a belief, observation, or contrarian insight → **Framework A**
- Topic is a story, experience, or turning point → **Framework B**

**Framework A — Harsh Truth + Contrast**
1. HOOK (1–3 lines): Harsh truth, paradox, or strong contrast. Pattern: "Everyone thinks X. It's actually Y."
2. OLD PATTERN + PAIN (3–6 lines): What most founders do and why it hurts. Concrete and relatable. Use "you" and "we".
3. REFRAME (2–3 lines): Flip the lens. At least one "It's not about X, it's about Y" sentence.
4. PRINCIPLES LIST (4–7 items): One concrete behavior shift per item. 1–2 lines each.
5. IDENTITY CLOSE (2–3 lines): Who they become if they apply this. Connect to scaling without hiring or personal transformation.
6. CTA: One specific question the reader can actually answer. Not "what do you think?"

**Framework B — Story → Insight → Lessons**
1. MINI STORY HOOK (2–4 lines): Scene with tension. Where, who, what unexpected thing happened.
2. MEANING / INSIGHT (3–5 lines): What this moment revealed. Plain language.
3. PRACTICAL LIST (3–6 numbered items): Concrete, behavior-level actions. One per line.
4. RETURN TO STORY (1–2 lines): Close the loop — what changed.
5. IDENTITY CLOSE + CTA (2–3 lines): Who this is for. One specific question.

## CTA Patterns (CTA-A through CTA-G)

Match to the post's assigned outcome. Vary across posts.

**CTA-A** · Lead magnet (LEAD_MAGNET):
If you want [thing]:
[1] Connect with [Name]
[2] Comment '[KEYWORD]'
and I'll send it over to you :)

**CTA-B** · Webinar/event:
[Day/date] [time] — drop your questions in the comments. [Cohost] is answering them live.

**CTA-C** · Product:
If you want to test it, we have a very generous free tier. Link in the comments.

**CTA-D** · None (EDUCATE / authority posts):
End with a soft genuine question, or no CTA at all. "What's your [X]?" is fine.

**CTA-E** · 24-hour link drop:
Comment '[KEYWORD]' and I'll send the [thing].
I'll share the link in 24 hrs.

**CTA-F** · Double-offer P.S.:
[main CTA in body]
P.S. We also [second resource]. Comment '[KEYWORD2]' if you want that one too. I'll DM both.

**CTA-G** · "You're still early" (KEYWORD_CTA / CONNECT):
Out of [big number], only [small %] are doing this.
You're still early to win.

## Content Strategy

- First line must be unmistakably clear. No wordplay, no ambiguity on line 1.
- Make one specific buyer feel diagnosed. Not "AI can help businesses grow." Instead: "Most founders don't have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem."
- Every post develops 3 main ideas, each broken into concrete subtopics.
- Rotate these core positioning phrases — do not repeat the same one in consecutive posts:
  "Scale without hiring more people" | "Remove manual work from pipeline" | "Turn AI into operational leverage" | "Build systems that don't forget" | "Automate follow-up before hiring another SDR"
