# 2.Human motivators

 

## a) Running away from pain - Current state

-For most humans , this is the most powerful motivator

“Change happens when the pain of staying the same is greater than the pain of change.” -make the pain of staying look bigger and the pain of change looking smaller

-You need to understand their Current State and the pain they are experiencing

### **You must ask yourself:**

- What is painful or frustrating in the current life of my avatar?
- What annoys them?
- What do they fear?
- What are they worrying about ?
- How do others negatively perceive them now?

## b)Running towards pleasure - Dream state

-For someone to change you must present a compelling, hope filled vision their future

-The more vivid and relevant to the the better.

### You must ask yourself

- If they could wave a magic wand at their life and fix everything, what would their life look like?
- What enjoyable new experiences would they have?
- How would others perceive them is a positive light?
- How would they fell about themselves if they made that change?

## The more vivid and dynamic you can be in your understanding of their pain and pleasure the more powerfully you can compel them to act.

## If you can encode the information/answers in senses - make them sensory - sight, sound, feel , smell , taste

# Long Form Sales Letter Basic Outline

# 1. Headline

The point of the headline is to grab attention, “break their brain”, and compel them to read more

# 2. Lead - first magnet shows them the problem and makes them think about it and then tease the solution

The point of the lead is to build rapport, amplify desire, and bribe the reader to continue reading

- Connect with the reader’s pain - make them feel relevant and understood
- Make a big promise - show them the dream state - make them feel it
- Tease mechanism - there is a way for them which you describe in the body
- Tease discovery story - they will discover something huge
- Establish credibility - show proof or/and sound professional

# 3. Body - shift their beliefs and show them the solution

The goal of the body is to shift their limiting beliefs, reveal the true nature of their problem and show them the mechanism or law of nature they need to follow to get what they want.

- Introduce Guru/Brand
- Preview height of drama
- Show struggles
- Show failed attempts to solve the problem
- A moment when all seems lost
- Decision and discovery of the law of nature
- Finding the solution/mechanism
- Experiencing the dream state

# 4. Close

The point of the close is to present the product and help them make their purchasing decision by stacking on their new beliefs

- Intro product - the vehicle that will help them do the solution and reach the dream state
- Show how the product taps into the mechanism to get the dream state
- Tease the contents of the product
- Testimonials
- Value stack and intro price - differentiate the price from what they get
- Stack additional value with bonuses and give them free stuff
- Guarantee/Risk reversal
- Scarcity/Urgency  - limited time , inventory
- 3 way close - You can do 1 or 2 or 3
- Additional Testimonial dump
