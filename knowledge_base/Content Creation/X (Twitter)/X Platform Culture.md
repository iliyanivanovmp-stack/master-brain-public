# X Platform Culture & Cadence

For a founder building in public on X, coming from LinkedIn. Compiled from 2026 algorithm research and stylistic analysis of @romanbuildsaas, @MichLieben, @itsalexvacca.

---

## Voice Register: X vs LinkedIn

LinkedIn rewards polish and "thrilled to share" cadence. X rewards the opposite — it reads as a group chat among practitioners.

**Lowercase is the default for founder Twitter.** Not a rule, a signal: "I'm typing on my phone between meetings, not running it through comms." Use lowercase for casual takes, shitposts, quick jokes. Use sentence case for gravitas: milestone tweets, hot takes, hard data. Mix is normal.

**What works on X:**
- Casual, specific, mildly self-roasting. "spent 3 weeks building what Claude did in 15 min from a single link."
- Hard numbers with no preamble. "Gojiberry AI just hit $2M ARR."
- Operator takes that imply scars. "After 3,200+ outbound campaigns the most expensive mistake we see is..."

**What kills reach:**
- "Excited to announce", "Thrilled to share", "Big news!" — instant skip
- Inspirational closers ("keep building, you got this") — LinkedIn import
- Vague reflection without numbers — specificity or nothing

The shitposting layer (1 in every 4-5 posts) is how you season the feed: one-line dunk on the industry, on yourself, on a screenshot. Pure jokes build the "real human" trust that makes people read the serious posts.

## Standalone Tweet Mechanics

- **Length:** 280-char ceiling. 71-100 char sweet spot for one-liners. 120-180 for context tweets. Full 280 chars gets ~71% lower engagement. If you can cut a word, cut it.
- **Punctuation:** Periods fine. Line breaks fine (1-2 max in a solo). No em dashes ever. No emoji bullets. Single inline emoji tolerated; emoji as decoration is not.
- **Numbered listicle solo tweets** work when each line is one short clause. 3-5 items max in a solo — more than that, make it a thread.
- **Reply ratio:** Reply within first 30-60 minutes to every commenter on your own tweets. Algorithm weights replies ~13.5x a like, and a reply-from-author chain ~150x a like.

---

## Threads

**When to use:** Idea genuinely needs 4-7 beats. 15-tweet mega-threads are dead.

- Hook tweet does 100% of the work. Must stand alone.
- Optimal length: 5-7 tweets.
- Last tweet is a CTA ("follow @handle for more on X" or "RT the first tweet if useful"). Threads without a closing CTA leave engagement on the floor.
- "1/" numbering optional — many high-performers skip it.

**Three thread archetypes:**
- **Story thread** — scar/failure → arc → lesson
- **Tactical thread** — step-by-step with screenshots and numbers per step
- **List thread** — each tweet = one item with one-line example

**Hashtags: zero.** Multiple hashtags get 40% reach penalty. Even one is neutral at best.

## LinkedIn Imports That Kill Reach

Any of these marks you as a tourist:
- "Excited to announce / Thrilled to share / Big news!"
- "Drop a 👇 in the comments" or "Agree?" closer
- "Here are 7 lessons from..." as standalone (works as thread hook, not solo)
- Emoji bullets (🚀 ✨ 💡 🎯) — inline emoji only, max one per tweet
- Hashtags. Any.
- Em dashes. Anywhere.
- Tagging 5+ people in one tweet (engagement bait)
- Carousel posts / slide-style screenshots — they don't exist on X

## Build-in-Public / GTM Founder Content Pillars

Five pillars, weighted roughly:

1. **Lessons learned (30%)** — specific, scarred, numbered. "after 3,200 campaigns: stop selling to the CMO."
2. **Milestone updates (20%)** — numbers with context. Small numbers are fine and relatable; share $1k MRR with same energy as $1M.
3. **Behind-the-scenes (20%)** — screenshots of dashboards, support replies, Loom thumbnails. Visuals as proof, not decoration.
4. **Struggles / self-roast (15%)** — "spent 3 weeks on a thing AI does in 15 min." Funny, specific, no pity.
5. **Hot takes (15%)** — industry observation other operators will quote-tweet.

**Specific numbers are the asset.** Round numbers ($2M ARR) for milestones. Granular numbers (93,605 emails, 90 demos) for weekly updates.

## Engagement Strategy

**Replies to bigger accounts are your single biggest growth lever.**
- 70/30 split: ~70% of time is replying to accounts 2-10x your follower count, ~30% is original posts
- Target 10-15 accounts (GTM founders, AI/outbound voices, your customers)
- Reply within 15 minutes of their posts with a specific data point or counter-angle
- Never "great post" or "this 🔥"

**Quote-tweet your own milestones** with new context ("update: 30 days later this is now $X").

## Cadence

- **3-5 original posts/day** under 1k followers. **5-10/day** from 1k-10k.
- Bursting beats spacing: 2-3 tweets in a 30-min window outperforms one per hour.
- **20-50 replies/day to bigger accounts** — this is the actual job.
- **One thread per week**, Tue-Thu 9-10am US Eastern for B2B audience.
- **Reply before you post** — get 10-20 replies live to warm up the algorithm before dropping your own tweet.
- **Don't quit at month 2** — most founders bail at week 8. Plan 6-month minimum before judging traction.
- **Pin the strongest thread** as your de facto bio.
