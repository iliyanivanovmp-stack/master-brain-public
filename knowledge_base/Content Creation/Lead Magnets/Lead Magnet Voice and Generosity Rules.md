# Lead Magnet Voice and Generosity Rules

## The generosity contract

Hard rules that hold across every format:

- **No gated middle.** The asset answers its own headline. Don't tease ("download to learn the 3 secrets"). Put the 3 secrets on the page.
- **Real prompts, real tools, real URLs, real numbers.** Verbatim. Inline the entire system prompt, the GitHub repo URL, the live pricing.
- **Working artifacts, not screenshots.** If the magnet promises a Notion template, link the public Notion URL. If it promises a GPT, link the GPT.
- **One soft CTA at the end, not interrupting.** Match topic to the CTA table below.
- **Distribution defaults to ungated** when "be generous" is the brief. Comment-keyword DMs are fine for launch post engagement, but the asset should also live at a public URL.

If a brief asks to gate the middle, push back once. Then comply.

## Voice registers

Two registers. Switch happens between sections, not mid-paragraph.

**AUTHOR voice** — the founder/operator voice. Personal. Story-led. Hook-driven. Use on: cover, opening, closing CTA.

**OPERATOR voice** — runbook register. Outcome-promise headers, imperative verbs, numerals, bullets, verbatim prompts. Use in: the body.

### Cover / opening (AUTHOR voice)

Default patterns (pull from `corpus.md` if available):
- **Concrete result + tool** ("85 sales calls. From 80,000 cold emails.")
- **Achievement + timeframe** ("How to book 14 sales calls in 2 weeks on LinkedIn without needing to go viral.")
- **R.I.P. [Role/Industry]** for vault drops
- **Stat opener with payoff** ("Last month I generated +110k impressions. Here's the profile system that converted them.")

### Body (OPERATOR voice)

- Section headers are outcome promises, not feature labels
- Numerals beat adjectives
- Imperative verbs. Drop hedges.
- One thought per paragraph. Most paragraphs 1-3 lines.
- Bullets carry the load. Pick `-`, `→`, or `•` and stay consistent within the magnet.
- Real screenshots / examples / prompts verbatim. No paraphrase.

### Close (AUTHOR voice)

Match topic to CTA table above. End with email + book-a-call link.

## Slop scrub (mandatory before publish)

Fix every hit before moving on.

### Punctuation

- No em dash (`—`). Hard ban. Replace with `.`, `,`, `:`, `()`, or `·`.
- No curly quotes. Straight only.
- No ellipsis (`…`). Use `...` if used at all.

### Banned words / phrases

delve · dive into · embark · unlock · unleash · harness · elevate · transform · revolutionize · foster · navigate · journey · landscape · realm · ecosystem · tapestry · paradigm · pivotal · crucial · vital · essential · meticulous · leverage · utilize · optimize · streamline · empower · amplify · spearhead · resonate · illuminate · shed light · beacon · testament · treasure trove · symphony · notably · arguably · indeed · in today's fast-paced world · at its core · it's worth noting · a testament to · game changer · move the needle · cutting-edge · state-of-the-art · seamless · world-class · scalable · robust · powerful · innovative · take a moment to · trust the process

**Fixes:** "use" not "leverage", "help" not "empower", "strong" not "robust", "big" not "transformative", "show" not "underscore", "many parts" not "multifaceted".

### Structural slop

- No TL;DR / Key takeaways / In summary headers. The magnet is the takeaway.
- Max one negation pivot per magnet ("It's not X. It's Y.").
- No throat-clearing transitions: Furthermore · Moreover · Additionally · That said · Importantly · Ultimately · Subsequently.
- No keycap emoji as numbered headers (1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣). Use plain `1)` or `Step 1`.
- No symmetric paragraphs (every paragraph the same length). Real paragraphs are uneven.

## Brand tokens (for designed surfaces — PDF, web tool, Sheet header)

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#fafafa` | page bg |
| `--surface` | `#ffffff` | cards, callouts |
| `--ink` | `#0a0a0a` | primary text + numerals |
| `--ink-soft` | `#3f3f46` | body copy |
| `--muted` | `#71717a` | captions, micro-labels, "em" half of headlines |
| `--line` | `#e4e4e7` | borders |
| `--accent` | `#10b981` | one accent per surface |

**One accent moment per surface**, used for meaning, not decoration.

For non-designed formats (Google Doc, plain GitHub README): don't brand the body. Brand stamps are the title, closing CTA, and link to aiessentials.us.

## Don'ts

- Don't default to PDF when content is more naturally Notion, a Sheet, a repo, or a GPT.
- Don't gate the middle.
- Don't use em dashes. Replace with periods, commas, colons, middot, or parens.
- Don't fabricate stats or client names.
- Don't ship without a canonical public URL.
- Don't promise URLs that don't exist yet.
- Don't ship a screenshot when you could ship the artifact.
- Don't write "TL;DR" or "key takeaways".
- Don't cross voice registers within a body section.
