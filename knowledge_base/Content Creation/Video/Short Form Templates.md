### **TEMPLATE OUTLINE -is it possible**

1. **Polarizing or thought-provoking question** 
- curiosity gap with  bold question

  Framework: Is it possible to be a [identity or belief] and still [desired achievement]?”

1. Interest Peak 
- Well yes, but it depends on this/these [number] thing/s.
2. **Personal context / credibility + stakes**
For the past [time duration], I’ve been [action/goal] with the purpose of [personal mission or motivator].
“I'm turning 21 this year and for the past 600 days I've been building my online business with the dream of retiring my Indonesian parents from their 9-5 job.”

3. **Internal conflict - reveal struggle . barrier, problem , enemy**
Framework: “But when I [major personal change or decision], things got complicated / difficult / conflicting.”

“But at one point in time, I decided to give my life to Jesus.
    
    And I'm not gonna lie bro, things started to get complicated.
    
    Why you ask? Because every day this one word kept on bothering me.”
    
4. **Question or fear that creates deeper introspection - state the deeper “what if ” - fear or worst case scenario you were in or you could be in**
Framework: “What if [feared scenario] is actually true? And even worse, what if [deeper, more personal fear]?”
“So I started thinking, what if making generational wealth is not a part of God's plan for me? Or even worse, what if I'm destined to work a mediocre nine to five, spend ten years running in a cubicle to pay off my uni debt, then another 30 years being a computer slave just to provide for my family?”

5. **Breakthrough insight / realization - the moment you discovered the key, truth , or the lie. the aha moment**
Framework: “Then I discovered this: [key insight, truth, or quote].”
Then I found this. God works for the good of those who love him. And also all of this too.”
6. **Shift in identity or perspective - show how this change your view, life, habits, identity.
Demonstrate it , show it!**
Framework: “Now I can [emotional state or mindset] every time I [new approach or behavior].”
****“Now I can experience a perfect peace every time I let go of the hustle.”
7. **Reframe  - connect the whole story to a larger pricniple belief or purpoise. Depths and meaning.**
Framework: “Because my identity isn’t in [old identity]. It’s in [new deeper identity, belief, or principle].”
Because my identity is not in entrepreneurship. It is in Christ alone. And plus, who's a better provider than the creator of the heavens and the earth himself?”
8.  **CTA (optional)** 
“And that was journal one of my new series, 21 by Grace. Can I get an amen, please?”
