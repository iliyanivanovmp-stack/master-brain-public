## Visual Style (for content creation)

**Overall aesthetic:** Minimal, clean, Apple-like - less is more. Every element earns its place.

- **Background/setting:** I shoot in many places as I am traveling constantly
- **Clothing style:** Old-money style - clean fits, neutral tones, understated
- **Editing style:** Fast cuts, clean and minimal - no flashy effects, no overloaded motion graphics
- **Thumbnail style:** Face + bold text, minimal elements, bright accent colors on clean backgrounds

### Design Principles (Apple-like)
- Lots of white space - never crowded
- Clean sans-serif typography (think SF Pro, Inter, or similar)
- Neutral base colors (white, off-white, light gray, black) with one accent color
- No gradients unless very subtle
- Icons and graphics: flat, simple, purposeful
- Text on screen: large, readable, minimal - one idea per frame

### On-screen text / captions style
- Clean font, high contrast (white on dark or black on light)
- Short lines - 3-5 words max per caption overlay
- No drop shadows, no outlines unless necessary for readability

### What to avoid
- Busy backgrounds or cluttered layouts
- Too many colors at once
- Comic Sans, decorative fonts, heavy textures
- Overuse of emojis in graphics
- "YouTuber bro" aesthetic - shocked faces, neon colors, chaos
