# Prospects Pre-Call Research

# Educ8theworld

## WHAT THEY DO

- Focus: **youth development (K-12 → early career)**
- Services include:
    - Curriculum design
    - Workshops & facilitation
    - Career + personal development
    - Strategic planning for orgs
    - Shaping characters
    - Personal development
    - Leadership
    - Community impact

## THEIR MISSION

### They sell **transformation**, not education.
You’re not offering programs. You’re oferring life direction.

WEAKNESESS:
Likely **manual delivery-heavy**
Likely **relationship-based growth

They WANT: Content Dev, Email Prospecting, Action Step Follow-Up**

## ICP

### **Institutions / organizations**

- Schools (K-12, colleges)
- Nonprofits
- Youth programs
- Government / community initiatives

### Secondary ICP - Individuals

- Students (teens / young adults)
- Early-stage professionals
- “Purpose-driven” people

## Weaknesses (important)

- Likely **not productized**
- Likely **manual delivery-heavy**
- Likely **relationship-based growth**
- Messaging is **inspirational, not conversion-focused**

 **“We want to build a system that turns your mission into revenue + scale”**

****

## System ideas

### A. Content Dev Engine (top of funnel) - Automatic SEO maybe

- Turn their philosophy into:
    - Daily posts
    - Email content
    - Lead magnets
- AI repurposing system

Outcome:

→ More inbound

→ Authority positioning

Automatic SEO

### B. Outbound System (pipeline builder)

- Target:
    - Schools
    - Nonprofits
    - Organizations
- Automated:
    - Lead scraping
    - Email sequences
    - Follow-ups

Outcome:

→ Predictable meetings

### C. Post-Workshop Monetization System (HUGE)

This is the hidden gold.

After every workshop:

- Automated follow-up emails
- Personalized action plans
- Upsells:
    - Advanced programs
    - Coaching
    - Membership

Outcome:

→ Turns **one-time workshops → recurring revenue**

---

### D. Participant Lifecycle Automation

- Onboarding → engagement → retention
- Progress tracking
- Nudges + accountability

Outcome:

→ Higher impact (they care about this)

## Wins for them

Emotional Win

- Less chaos
- More control
- Feels scalable

### Business win

- More booked programs
- More repeat clients
- More monetization per participant

### Real win (your framing)

> “Every workshop you run should keep making you money after it ends.”
> 

## Smart questions to ask on the call

Use these to dominate the conversation:

### Revenue clarity

- “Where does most of your revenue come from right now?”

### Bottleneck identification

- “What happens after someone attends a workshop?”

### Pipeline

- “How are you currently getting new schools or organizations?”

### Scale limiter

- “What breaks if you double your clients tomorrow?”

### Follow-up gap

- “Do you have a structured follow-up system after programs?”
