The “HSO” framework is a way to tell a story that will connect with the reader and make him interested and engaged.The story should be written from a random person as like he is telling it to his friends/ The story should have drama. The first part is Hook In the Hook the point is to grab the reader attention and make him want to read the story. The Hook should contain a title and one to two sentences more maximum or the title only. The Hook should start at the height of drama- the most dramatic part of the story. The second part is the Story: The Story should be about the current state of the IDC that you came up with and his problems . When you are telling a story ,start at the HIGHT OF DRAMA , make the main character relatable to the IDC - make the reader seem himself in the hero.You can lead the reader to the path of despair of the hero - the current state of the IDC that you created - the tragic state that the IDC is in basically. And then shift forward in time to the dream state of the IDC that you created - so the reader can wonder what the hero of the story did to reach the dream state of the IDC. You should basically create an information gap from the Current State to the Dream State which will make the reader very curious to discover the missing information. But don’t present it as some kind of a secret. It should not look like you are trying to hide the missing information from the reader. The third part of the story is the Offer In the Offer you need to direct the reader to take action to discover the information in the information gap that you created in the Story. Now we will proceed with Lesson 3 of the second stage of the Writing Phase - Long Form Sales Letter
Here is Lesson 3 - how to write  Long Form Sales Letter:

1.Headline
The Headline is the most important part.
The point of the headline is grab attention, “break their brain”, and compel them to read more
-A big, bold promise related to the IDC’s biggest pain or desire.
-Create curiosity and compel them to keep reading.
-Think of it as a one-sentence summary of why they should care

2.Lead
The point of the lead is to build rapport, amplify desire, and bribe the reader to continue reading
-Grab attention immediately with a story, shocking statistic, or bold statement.
-Introduce the IDC’s pain or desire in a way that feels personal and relatable.
-Set the stage for the solution.
-Connect with the reader’s pain
-Make a big promise
-Tease mechanism
-Tease discovery story
-Establish credibility

3.Problem(agitate the pain)
The goal of the body is to shift their limiting beliefs, reveal the true nature of their problem and show them the mechanism or law of nature they need to follow to get what they want.

- Introduce Guru/Brand
-Preview height of drama
-Dive deeply into the IDC’s Current State.
-Describe their struggles and frustrations in vivid, emotional detail.
-Show failed attempts to solve the problem
-Moment when all seems lost
-Show that you truly understand their pain.
-Hint that there’s a way out without giving away the full solution yet.
-Finding the solution/mechanism

4.Solution
-Paint a vivid picture of the IDC’s Dream State.
-Describe how life can look and feel once their problem is solved.
-Build anticipation by introducing the product as the key to achieving that outcome.

5.Product
The point of the close is to present the product, and help them make their purchasing decision by stacking on their new beliefs

- Introduce the product or offer with clear details.
-Explain what the product does and how it works.
-Show how product taps into the mechanism to get the Dream State
-Tease contents of product
-Highlight the features, but focus more on benefits (how it improves the IDC’s life)
-Use bullets or fascinations to break down the value

6.Proof and testimonials
-Show testimonials, case studies, or success stories.
-Highlight credentials, awards, or personal experience to demonstrate expertise.
-Use data, charts, or visuals if applicable to back up claims.
-Value stack and intro price
-Stack additional value with bonuses

7.Overcome objections
-Address potential doubts or hesitations the IDC might have.
-Use empathy to acknowledge their concerns and logic to counter them.

1. Call to Action (CTA)
-Create a clear, strong call to action.
-Remind the IDC of the pain they’re escaping and the Dream State they’re chasing.
-Add urgency and scarcity when appropriate (e.g., limited-time offer or spots available).

9.Guarantee
-Guarantee/Risk reversal
-Offer a risk-free guarantee to reduce hesitation.
-For example, a 30-day money-back guarantee or "try it free for X days."
-Scarcity/Urgency
-Make it urgent by installing a feeling of scarcity or urgency.

10.Close
-End with a heartfelt, motivational push.
-Reiterate the promise of transformation and encourage immediate action.
