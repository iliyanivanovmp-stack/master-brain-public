“I’m not interested.” (Tier 2)	“Got it. Is that because there’s no budget in place or are there just other initiatives taking priority right now?” (Force a Tier 1 objection you can work with.)

“Take me off your list.” (Tier 2)	Same Hail Mary if they’re at least talking: “Got it. Is that because there’s no budget in place or are there just other initiatives taking priority right now?” Otherwise treat as a hard no and move on.

“We already use X, so I don’t see why it makes sense to meet.”	“Got it. Yeah, that actually is why I was reaching out. We work with customers of [X] all the time, so if there’s ever a fit, we can move ahead, but I still think it at least makes sense for us to align and introduce you to my team even if it’s just 15 minutes, because we’ll be the main ones supporting you in this area or any other priorities you have in these areas going forward. So does later this week work, Thursday or Friday?”

“We just implemented X; contract not up for 3 years, so I don’t see why it makes sense to meet.”	“Got it, that makes sense. And when you say you’re using Workday, is that Adaptive for planning? The reason I ask is we work with a lot of Workday customers and usually yes, they’re using it for planning, but then they’re using BlackLine for account recs and financial close, Workiva for financial reporting, Power BI for analytics. I don’t know what your tech stack looks like, but there’s usually an opportunity to consolidate tooling and deliver a pretty meaningful ROI in the process, which is the reason I ask. That’s why I wanted to set up a call. I’m sure you’re probably busy right now and I definitely want to introduce you to my team too, and we can go into more detail on this. Does next Thursday or Friday work better?”

“We don’t have budget right now.”	“Got it. Yeah, this wouldn’t be a conversation around spend. This would more be to get aligned with your priorities going forward so that when budget does open up, we’re ready to jump in and support right away. So does next Thursday or Friday work for you?”

“Other initiatives are taking priority.”	“Got it. Yeah, this isn’t about kicking off a big project right now. It’d be more to align with your priorities so when those initiatives wrap, we already understand your landscape and can support quickly. Does later next week work, maybe Wednesday or Thursday?”

(Follow‑up to budget / priorities objections – ACE)	“Got it, that makes sense. Curious, what do those initiatives look like? Is it more around finance transformation, compliance? I’m just trying to understand how an ERP might fit into this in the future. The reason I ask is we see a lot of teams delay planning conversations until budget is live, and then implementation lags them another 6–12 months. Sometimes just getting aligned early shortens that gap a lot. That’s why I wanted to at least get 20–30 minutes on the books with you and my team. Would Wednesday afternoon or Thursday morning work?”

“Send me an email.” (soft brush‑off)	“Totally, I can send something over. Usually what works best is a quick 15‑minute intro so we can tailor what we send based on your setup instead of a generic overview. How does Wednesday or Thursday afternoon look?”

“Send me an email.” (they’re clearly busy / in a line)	“No worries, sounds like you’re in the middle of something. Let me shoot you a quick email and, just so we don’t play calendar tag, why don’t we pencil a 15‑minute intro for next week? Is Tuesday or Wednesday better, and I’ll include it in the invite?”

Hard hangup / “you’re a scammer”	Treat as a hard no; no live rebuttal. Use it as a signal to work on opener, value statement, tonality, and confidence if this happens a lot.
